About TechMinds

Start to finish.

We started TechMinds because too many businesses get handed between sales, design and development teams who’ve never spoken to each other.

Our story

The people who scope it, build it.

TechMinds is an IT consulting practice built around a simple idea: the people who scope your project should be the same people who design it, build it and keep it running.

No hand-offs. No “that was the last team’s decision”. No agency churn. We work with businesses that need custom software and the cloud infrastructure underneath it — and want a partner who stays accountable after launch.

  • Senior by default

    Every engagement is staffed by senior engineers and architects — not routed to whoever’s free.

  • Accountable end to end

    The team that designs your system is the team that ships and supports it.

  • Plain-language scoping

    Fixed-scope proposals you can actually read, with milestones that mean something.
